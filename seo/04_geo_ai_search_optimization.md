# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Search Blueprint

As search transitions to **AI-First Answer Engines** (ChatGPT Web Search, Claude 3.7 Sonnet Search, Perplexity Pro, Google AI Overviews), traditional SEO must be augmented with **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**.

---

## 1. What is GEO for Vyor AI?

When a user asks:
> *"What are the best enterprise platforms for building autonomous neural agents with real-time SDKs?"*
> *"Which AI Virtual Try-On SDK offers sub-second diffusion latency?"*

AI models synthesize answers by reading authoritative, structured, and factual web sources. GEO optimizes Vyor AI to be the **#1 cited authority**.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Vyor AI GEO

### Pillar 1: `llms.txt` Standard
The `llms.txt` file is located at the root of `https://vyorai.com/llms.txt`. It gives LLM crawlers a clean, Markdown-formatted summary of Vyor AI’s product lines, API endpoints, benchmarks, and enterprise use cases.

### Pillar 2: Passage-Level Citability
AI models extract direct factual sentences. We structure key copy with:
- Clear declarative claims (e.g. *"Vyor AI processes autonomous neural agent decisions with sub-15ms latency across global edge nodes."*)
- Bulleted technical capabilities.
- Unambiguous entity relationships (Vyor AI -> Infinity SDK, Vyor CRM, AI Virtual Try-On).

### Pillar 3: AI Bot Access in `robots.txt`
Explicitly allow `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, and `Google-Extended` to crawl without rate-limiting hurdles.
