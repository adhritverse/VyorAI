# Schema.org Structured Data Specifications

Structured data enables Google and Bing to understand the entities, capabilities, products, and organizational hierarchy of **Vyor AI**, unlocking **Knowledge Graph Panels**, **Software Rich Snippets**, and **FAQ Accordion Rich Results** on Google SERPs.

---

## 1. Schema Graphs Implemented

### Graph 1: `Organization`
Identifies Vyor AI as an enterprise AI software organization, its official URLs, contact points, and social profiles.

### Graph 2: `WebSite`
Defines the primary domain, site search potential, and brand name.

### Graph 3: `SoftwareApplication` (Vyor AI Engine & Infinity SDK)
Establishes application category (`BusinessApplication`, `DeveloperApplication`), operating system compatibility, and pricing model.

### Graph 4: `FAQPage`
Enhances search listings with interactive expandable FAQ question/answer snippets directly in Google SERP.

---

## 2. Validation & Testing

All schemas adhere strictly to [Schema.org](https://schema.org) standards and pass the [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) with **0 Errors** and **0 Warnings**.

See [`02_schema_markup.json`](./02_schema_markup.json) for the full raw JSON-LD payload.
